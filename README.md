# Tech Job Market & Salary Analysis Using Data Science

## Horizon TechX Internship – Task 3

### Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a technology job market dataset to understand patterns and trends in technology-sector employment and salaries.

The analysis focuses on job roles, experience levels, employment types, work models, company sizes, company locations, yearly salary trends, and relationships between numerical variables.

## Objectives

- Understand the structure and characteristics of the dataset
- Check data quality, missing values, and duplicate records
- Analyze technology job roles and job demand
- Study salary distributions and salary differences
- Compare salaries across experience levels
- Analyze remote, hybrid, and on-site work models
- Compare salary patterns across company sizes and locations
- Examine yearly salary trends
- Identify important patterns and insights from the data

## Dataset

The dataset contains 6,599 job records and 11 features.

Main features include:

- job_title
- experience_level
- employment_type
- work_models
- work_year
- employee_residence
- salary
- salary_currency
- salary_in_usd
- company_location
- company_size

For salary comparisons, `salary_in_usd` is used as the standardized salary measure.

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains 6,599 job records and 11 features.
- No missing values or duplicate records were found.
- The overall average salary is approximately $145,561.
- Experience level has an important relationship with salary.
- Data Engineer, Data Scientist, and Data Analyst are among the commonly represented job roles.
- Salary levels vary across work models, company sizes, and locations.
- Salary trends change across different years.

## Conclusion

This exploratory data analysis provides a data-driven view of the technology job market by examining job roles, experience levels, salaries, work models, company sizes, locations, and yearly trends.

The project demonstrates how Python-based data analysis and visualization can be used to identify meaningful patterns and insights from real-world job market data.
